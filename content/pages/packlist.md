# Essentials

- **Shelter**
	- [ ] Tent (check pegs!)
	- [ ] Ground sheet
	- [ ] Sleeping bag
	- [ ] Inflatable mattress (check repair kit!)
- **Hydration**
	- [ ] Water (drinking, cooking, washing, coffee)
	- [ ] Water purification drops
- **Food & cooking**
    - [ ] Food (see [[#Food]] below)
	- [ ] Small pot & lid
	- [ ] Bowls & cutlery (bowl may not be needed if eating from bags)
	- [ ] Metal cup
	- [ ] Camping stove & gas (check gas level!)
	- [ ] Spare matches (in Ziploc!)
	- [ ] Waste bag
- **Clothing**
	- [ ] Rain jacket
	- [ ] Puffer jacket / fleece
	- [ ] Zip-off long pants
	- [ ] 1x Exercise shorts (for after / during swimming / bathing)
	- [ ] Breathable shirts
	- [ ] Buff and/or beanie
	- [ ] Sun hat
	- [ ] Shoes & socks (+1 pair for use at camp)
	- [ ] Underwear (+1 for sleeping)
	- [ ] Thermal base layer(s) for sleeping
- **Extra rain & sun protection**
	- [ ] Backpack rain cover
	- [ ] Sunblock
	- [ ] Sunglasses
	- [ ] Dry bags (for toilet paper, sleeping equipment, warm clothes)
	- [ ] Waterproof survival bag (for river crossings, if applicable)
- **Emergency**
	- [ ] Map (Slingsby maps are good; and/or GPS watch; check charge & load route file!)
	- [ ] First aid items (see [[#First aid]] below)
	- [ ] Charged phone (for emergencies; remember to charge on commute!)
	- [ ] Rehydrat sachets
- **Illumination**
	- [ ] Headlight
	- [ ] Batteries (check charge!)
- **Toiletries**
	- [ ] Towel
	- [ ] Toilet paper (with cover!)
	- [ ] Toothbrush & toothpaste
	- [ ] Deodorant
	- [ ] Biodegradable soap (humans & dishes)
- [ ] **Backpack**
- [ ] **Hiking permit**

# Food

- **Any-time snacks (no cooking)**
	- [ ] Dried banana chips
	- [ ] Guava roll / dried fruit
	- [ ] Nuts
	- [ ] Jungle bars / Far Bars
	- [ ] Zha cai / other bags of pickled vegetable (from Asian grocers)
	- [ ] Corn nibs (!!)
	- [ ] Oat cookies (!!)
	- [ ] Oreos
	- [ ] Rice crackers & spread
	- [ ] Naartjies and/or apples
	- [ ] Brownies
	- [ ] Sandwiches
	- [ ] Mushroom / seitan biltong
	- [ ] Woolworths gelatin-free gums / sour worms
- **Breakfasts**
	- [ ] Instant ramen (brands more substantial than Maggi, eg. Nongshim)
	- [ ] Oats (premixed with toasted seeds, coconut flakes, oat milk powder, sugar, chia seeds)
    - [ ] Nut butter sachets (for mixing with oats)
	- [ ] Tea bags & instant coffee (sachets with creamer); ≥1 per morning
	- [ ] Instant porridge like ProNutro / sorghum
- **Dinners**
	- [ ] Freeze-dried meals
		- [ ] *Forever Fresh* (Orca in Claremont has specials on these)
		- [ ] *Trek ‘n Eat* (check online; allegedly no longer imported)
		- [ ] [Dri Food Co](https://drifoodco.com/) in Bothasig (also sell individual freeze-dried ingredients)
	- [ ] Instant ramen (brands more substantial than Maggi, eg. Nongshim)
		- [ ] Dried additives for ramen, eg. shiitake, wakame, nutritional yeast, etc
	- [ ] Instant soup (eg. cup-a-soup)
	- [ ] Smash instant mash
	- [ ] Soy mince (instant) with mieliepap (premix with salt!)
	- [ ] Cous cous
	- [ ] Dried red lentils (premixed with spices!); takes longer to cook
	- [ ] Instant tteok-bokki
	- [ ] Instant chai latte powder sachets for dessert (!!)

# First aid

## Main checklist

- [ ] Space blanket
- [ ] Ibuprofen tablets (several, for headache / muscle pain)
- [ ] Plasters & bandages
- [ ] Extra personal medication (like asthma pump, allergy pills, etc)
- [ ] Period sanitation items

## Full stock list

- [ ] Scissors (5.5cm blade)
- [ ] Micro-porous tape (5m)
- [ ] Pack of safety pins
- [ ] 2 pairs of nitrile gloves
- [ ] Tweezers
- [ ] Crepe bandage (5cm x 450cm)
- [ ] Woven bandage (7.5cm x 500cm)
- [ ] 4 cleansing wipes
- [ ] Burn gel sachet (3.5g)
- [ ] 5 gauze swabs (5cm x 5cm)
- [ ] Small low adherent dressing (5cm x 5cm)
- [ ] Large low adherent dressing (10cm x 10cm)
- [ ] Sterile adherent dressing (16cm x 8cm)
- [ ] Pack of assorted plasters
- [ ] 2 blister plasters
- [ ] 4 ibuprofen tablets
