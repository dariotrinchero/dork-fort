# Don't worry, that's just Baraka!

![[baraka.jpg]]

Baraka is a HeroRAT: an African giant pouched rat, trained by [APOPO](https://apopo.org)
to sniff out landmines. He's light enough not to set them off, ignores scrap metal, & can
search a tennis court's worth of ground in half an hour (a technician with a metal detector
might take four days). I'm one of his adopters.
